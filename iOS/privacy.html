Peace Privacy Policy

Your data should feel private because it is.

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Peace is designed to help you track mood patterns, keep short notes, and access premium reflections without turning your emotional data into advertising inventory.

Core mood entries, notes, and recent chat history are stored locally on your device. The AI chat runs on-device with Apple Intelligence when available. Only if it is not available, and only after you give permission in the app, chat messages are sent to Google Gemini to generate replies.

1. What Peace stores

Peace can store the following information directly on your device:

  • mood check-ins and intensity values;
  • notes you write alongside a daily entry;
  • recent premium chat history so you can resume conversations;
  • basic app preferences such as language, reminders, and premium state cache.

2. AI features

Peace prefers on-device processing for premium emotional analysis through Apple Foundation Models when those models are available on the device and enabled by Apple.

If Apple Intelligence is not available on your device, the premium AI chat can use Google Gemini, an AI service provided by Google LLC. Peace asks for your explicit permission before sending anything to Google. If you do not allow it, nothing is sent and the chat will not reply on that device.

When you allow it, the following data is sent to Google each time you send a chat message:

  • the messages you write in the chat and the previous messages of the same conversation;
  • a short summary of your recent mood (for example the overall trend and energy level).

Peace does not send your name, your individual daily notes, or any account identifiers to Google. Google processes this data to generate the reply, under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can withdraw your permission at any time in Settings > AI chat with Google Gemini.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Premium purchases are processed by Apple StoreKit. Peace does not receive your full payment card details. Apple may share transaction data that is necessary to confirm whether your account has an active subscription.

4. Notifications

If you enable reminders, Peace uses Apple notification APIs to schedule a local reminder on your device. Notification permission can be changed at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Data sharing

Peace does not sell your personal data and does not use third-party advertising SDKs inside the app.

The only third party that may receive your content is Google, and only for the AI chat as described in section 2, after you give permission.

Your data may still be included in Apple device backups or iCloud backups if those features are enabled on your Apple account.

6. Your control

You can delete mood entries from inside the app when supported, or remove the app entirely from your device. Removing the app also removes its locally stored data from that device.

7. Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact the developer through the official Peace support channel listed in the App Store or repository associated with the app release.
