    <p><strong>Last Updated: December 7, 2025</strong></p>

    <div class="highlight">

        <strong>The "On-Device" Guarantee:</strong> Peace is built using <strong>Apple Intelligence Foundation Models</strong>. This means that your conversations and emotional data are processed locally on your iPhone.

    <h2>1. Data Collection & Storage</h2>

    <p>Peace does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to external servers. All mood logs, chat histories, and reflections are stored <strong>locally and exclusively on your device</strong>.</p>


    <h2>2. Artificial Intelligence Processing</h2>

    <p>The AI support features in Peace utilize on-device machine learning. No personal identifiers or chat contents are shared with third-party AI providers when using the native Foundation Models.</p>


    <h2>3. Third-Party Access</h2>

    <p>We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties. Your data never leaves your device unless you manually choose to export or backup your device via iCloud (subject to your Apple iCloud settings).</p>


    <h2>4. Data Retention</h2>

    <p>Since your data is stored locally, you have total control over it. Deleting the app will permanently remove all locally stored data.</p>


    <h2>5. Contact</h2>
